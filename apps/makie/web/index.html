Welcome to Makie

  1. Add shapes — click any shape on the left, then click on the bed. Drag shapes in too.
  2. Move & resize — drag a part to slide it, use the coloured arrows to move up/down, or type exact sizes on the right.
  3. Make holes — set a shape to Hole, then Group it with a solid. The hole is cut out, just like Tinkercad.
  4. Print — press Export to save an STL or 3MF for your slicer.

Mouse: left-drag empty space or right-drag to orbit · middle-drag or Shift+right-drag to pan · wheel to zoom · press ? for all shortcuts.

Parts
Drag a part onto another to group them. Drag it out to ungroup.
