> ⚠️ **Tinkerie is in alpha.** Everything works, but it's still being tested on real hardware, so expect rough edges and changes. Please [report](https://github.com/normanlove/tinkerie/issues) anything that doesn't work.

Free maker tools by **nrmluvdev**: Wirie, Makie, Tunie, Flashie, Scopie, Meterie, Probie and Codie in one program.

### Which file?
- **Windows 10/11:** `Tinkerie-0.22.0-alpha-windows.exe` (just run it), or `Tinkerie-0.22.0-alpha-windows.zip` if your connection blocks .exe downloads (it also has the source)
- **Mac:** `…-mac-apple-silicon.zip` (M1 or newer) or `…-mac-intel.zip`
- **Linux PC:** `…-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`
- **Raspberry Pi:** `…-pi-64bit.tar.gz` (64-bit Raspberry Pi OS) or `…-pi-32bit.tar.gz`

Each package has a *READ ME FIRST.txt*.

### New in 0.22.0-alpha
- **Auto-fix mode no longer loops:** it never runs twice on the same problems. Anything it can't fix stays in the list (with what to try) until you change the board, and then it has another go.
- **Tracks that stayed too close now get fixed:** when a redrawn track lands too close again (usually a diagonal cutting a corner), each round leaves a little more room round tracks, redraws both tracks of a clashing pair, and as a last resort makes a wide power track normal width.
- **Auto-fix is much faster:** it only re-routes the connections that need it, and stops as soon as another round wouldn't help.
