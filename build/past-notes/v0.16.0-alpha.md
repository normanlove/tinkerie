> ⚠️ **Tinkerie is in alpha.** Everything works, but it's still being tested on real hardware, so expect rough edges and changes. Please [report](https://github.com/normanlove/tinkerie/issues) anything that doesn't work.

Free maker tools by **nrmluvdev**: Wirie, Makie, Tunie, Flashie, Scopie, Meterie, Probie and Codie in one program.

### Which file?
- **Windows 10/11:** `Tinkerie-0.16.0-alpha-windows.exe` (just run it), or `Tinkerie-0.16.0-alpha-windows.zip` if your connection blocks .exe downloads (it also has the source)
- **Mac:** `…-mac-apple-silicon.zip` (M1 or newer) or `…-mac-intel.zip`
- **Linux PC:** `…-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`
- **Raspberry Pi:** `…-pi-64bit.tar.gz` (64-bit Raspberry Pi OS) or `…-pi-32bit.tar.gz`

Each package has a *READ ME FIRST.txt*.

### New in 0.16.0-alpha
- **Keebie:** part spacing (tight, comfortable, roomy) so diode rows don't crowd; new diode patterns (zig-zag, wave, arch, chevron, slant, smile, alternating, herringbone, staggered); the "little map" layout was removed.
- **Through-hole by default:** 1N4148 diodes, 6×6mm reset button, SS12D00 power switch, 3mm LEDs and leaded resistors, picked from the most common parts.
- **Power and activity lights**, knobs and a screen (now also 1.3in) on any keyboard, with a one-click switch to a controller with enough pins.
- **Standoffs** (M2, M2.5 or M3) placed for you or by hand; controllers show which end their USB socket is; a **parts list** for the whole keyboard (every board) and for any PCB.
