> ⚠️ **Tinkerie is in alpha.** Everything works, but it's still being tested on real hardware, so expect rough edges and changes. Please [report](https://github.com/normanlove/tinkerie/issues) anything that doesn't work.

Free maker tools by **nrmluvdev**: Wirie, Makie, Tunie, Flashie, Scopie, Meterie, Probie and Codie in one program.

### Which file?
- **Windows 10/11:** `Tinkerie-0.18.0-alpha-windows.exe` (just run it), or `Tinkerie-0.18.0-alpha-windows.zip` if your connection blocks .exe downloads (it also has the source)
- **Mac:** `…-mac-apple-silicon.zip` (M1 or newer) or `…-mac-intel.zip`
- **Linux PC:** `…-linux-x86_64.tar.gz`
- **Raspberry Pi:** `…-pi-64bit.tar.gz` (64-bit Raspberry Pi OS) or `…-pi-32bit.tar.gz`

Each package has a *READ ME FIRST.txt*.

### New in 0.18.0-alpha
- **Look & accessibility in every app:** a Look button (or Ctrl+,) with Dark, Light, High contrast and Colour-blind safe themes, text and controls size (90–150%), touch-friendly controls, reduced motion, an extra-strong focus outline, easier-to-read text and underlined links. Meterie has the themes and reduced motion on its Settings page.
- **Touchscreens:** on drawing areas (Wirie's board, Keebie, Scopie, Probie, Makie and more) drag with a finger, press and hold for the right-click menu, pinch to zoom and use two fingers to pan. Bigger buttons when touch is on.
- **Tidier on smaller screens:** Wirie's mode tabs become icons, and Makie's and Scopie's toolbars wrap instead of running off the edge; side panels narrow on tablets.
- **Teaching mode** says it's on the first time only, instead of every time an app opens. Icon-only buttons now have names for screen readers.
